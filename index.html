Television Market Analysis

Detailed breakdown of screen technologies, efficiency ratings, and usage statistics in Australia.

The type of screen technology that are currently available in Australia are LCD, LCD (LED) and OLED.

Average annual energy consumption increases proportionately with screen size across all panel types in the Australian market.

Higher star-rated televisions significantly lower ongoing electricity costs, saving up to 40% annually compared to lower-rated models.

Modern televisions feature ultra-low standby power modes, meeting strict Australian Greenhouse and Energy Standards (GEMS).
